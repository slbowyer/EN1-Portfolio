Activity 2: Object Redesign

We were tasked with using AI to redesign an object. We were given 2 cards at random one was a fishing rod and one was heavy/unmovable. We had to combine these 2 cards, so our creation (with the help of AI) was the FishTank™. The way the FishTank™ works is by shooting a fish that swims by with a little metal magnet, then it turns on an electromagnet to draw the fish into the tank, then, after enough fish have been captured in the tank, the electromagnet turns off and the fish are sucked up a chute, and finally the chute drops the fish onto a conveyor belt that brings the fish straight to you!
